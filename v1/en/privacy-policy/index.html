Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

We handle the information you entrust to us with care. Below you can read which data we process, why, for how long and what your rights are.

Who is responsible?

DenVick BV, Klein Mechelen 18b/9, 2880 Bornem, Belgium.

VAT BE 0768.417.469. Email: info@denvick.be. Phone: +32 484 136 748.

DenVick BV is responsible for processing your personal data as described in this policy.

Which data do we process?

Through the quote form: your name, phone number, email address, the service you choose and what you tell us about your project.

When you email, call or message us on WhatsApp: the information you share yourself, such as your name, number, the site address and photos.

On every visit to the website: technical data the server records automatically, such as your IP address, the time and the page requested.

What do we use it for?

To answer your question, plan a site visit and prepare a quote.

To carry out the works, invoice them and remain available for aftercare.

To keep the website secure and working properly.

We do not use your data for newsletters or advertising, and we never sell it.

On what legal basis?

We process your data to prepare a quote at your request and to perform a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), to meet legal obligations such as accounting law (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR), and on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the website secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who receives your data?

Only the people at DenVick who handle your request. We also work with a few service providers that use the data only to deliver their service:

one.com: hosting of the website and our mailbox, with servers in the European Union.

CallMeBot and WhatsApp (Meta): when you send a request through the form, we send a short notification with your name, phone number and the chosen service to our own WhatsApp, so we can respond quickly. These services may process data outside the European Union.

WhatsApp (Meta): when you contact us on WhatsApp yourself.

We do not pass your data to anyone else unless the law requires us to.

How long do we keep it?

Requests that do not lead to an assignment are kept for no more than two years.

If you become a client, we keep your data for as long as needed for the works and aftercare. Invoices and accounting records are kept for seven years, as required by Belgian law.

Server logs are deleted automatically by our hosting provider after a short period.

Your rights

You can always access your data, have it corrected or have it erased. You can also ask us to restrict processing, object to it or receive your data in a common format.

To do so, email info@denvick.be. We reply within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority, Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be.

Security

The website uses a secure connection (HTTPS). Only the staff who handle your request have access to your data.

Cookies

This website does not place any cookies. Read more in our cookie policy.

Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we start using new services. The date at the top shows the latest version.
