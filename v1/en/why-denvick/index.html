About us

An established name in construction

DenVick is a reliable guide for anyone who wants their project carried out to a high standard. For more than 15 years our expertise and experience have served every client, private or professional.

We deliver tailored work: expert advice, quality materials properly installed and the aftercare and support your project needs. Our VCA certified and motivated people are ready for every challenge.

Successful projects and good client relationships have made DenVick an established name in construction, across the Benelux.
