Our own fitters, our own responsibility

A structure is only as good as its assembly. A girder a few millimetres out of position, an anchor that is not properly set or a base plate that does not bear fully can cause problems later. That is why we do not leave assembly to changing subcontractors, but carry it out with our own team of experienced fitters. They know our way of working, our safety rules and our quality standards.

Precise work in existing buildings

Much assembly work does not take place on an open site, but in a building that is already in use: a basement, a lift shaft or a production hall. There, every centimetre counts. With compact equipment, careful preparation and clear agreements with the building’s users, we work precisely without disrupting daily operations more than necessary.

From anchoring to finishing

Because we also drill, grout and coat, we take on the entire assembly. We drill the anchor holes, install the anchors, position and align the components, grout the base plates and finish the connections. You have a single point of contact and the assurance that every step is coordinated.

Working safely

Assembly work often takes place at height, with heavy components and close to other activities. All our fitters work to the VCA safety standard. Work zones are screened off, lifting operations are prepared in advance and every assembly starts with clear agreements on access and safety.

Our approach

How an assembly job runs

Good assembly starts long before the first bolt: with a clear plan, the right preparation and agreements on access and safety.

  1. 01

    Preparation

    We review the drawings, the site and the access, and coordinate the schedule with the other parties.

  2. 02

    Setting out

    We set out the positions precisely, so that every component ends up exactly in its place.

  3. 03

    Anchoring

    Drilling anchor holes and installing chemical or mechanical anchors in concrete and masonry.

    Concrete drilling and sawing
  4. 04

    Assembly and alignment

    The components are positioned, aligned and connected according to plan.

  5. 05

    Grouting

    Base plates and pedestals are grouted underneath, so that they bear fully and stably.

    Grouting
  6. 06

    Inspection and handover

    We check dimensions and connections and hand over the assembly in good order.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about assembly works

Your question is not listed? Ask us directly.

Do you also assemble structures made by others?

Yes. Alongside our own structures, we also assemble components supplied by third parties, according to their drawings and specifications.

Do you work in existing buildings?

Yes, and that is often the largest part of our work. With compact equipment, we also work in basements, shafts and spaces that remain in use.

How is anchoring into concrete done?

With chemical or mechanical anchors, chosen according to the load and the substrate. If there is any doubt about the concrete, we check its quality first.

Do your fitters work to VCA?

Yes. All our crews work to the VCA safety standard, including on hazardous sites and in industrial environments.

Can you carry out assembly at short notice?

That depends on the scope and our schedule. Get in touch and we will look at what is possible together.

How much do assembly works cost?

That depends on the scope, the accessibility and the equipment required. After a site visit, you will receive a clear, free quote with no obligation.
