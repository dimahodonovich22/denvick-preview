Warning signs

Do you recognise this?

A flat roof rarely starts leaking overnight. Problems usually announce themselves. Acting in time avoids water damage inside and a complete roof renovation.

Water after a rain shower

Damp patches on the ceiling, drips along a rooflight or water coming in during heavy rain.

Blisters and wrinkles

Bubbles, loose seams or wrinkles in the roof covering point to moisture under the membrane or an ageing system.

Standing water

Water still on the roof days after rain, caused by deflection of the roof or a blocked drain.

Cracks at the edges

Cracked connections at upstands, roof edges and penetrations, exactly where most leaks start.

A flat roof works every day

A flat roof has a lot to endure. Rain stays on it longer than on a pitched roof, the sun heats up the roof covering and frost makes it contract again. That constant expansion and contraction puts particular strain on the seams and connections. Over time small openings form there, and once water gets under the roof covering, it spreads unseen across the roof.

Repair or renovate

Not every leak calls for a new roof. If the roof covering is otherwise sound, we repair the leak or the detail where things go wrong. If there is water in the insulation, the covering is worn out or the roof leaks in several places, a renovation makes more sense. We tell you honestly what your roof really needs.

The right roof covering for your roof

Bitumen, EPDM or a liquid roofing system: each has its strengths. Bitumen is robust and proven on large roofs. EPDM is flexible and resistant to sun and frost. A liquid roofing system forms a seamless layer that is easy to connect around penetrations, rooflights and installations. For roof terraces and roofs that are walked or driven on, we combine the waterproofing with a wear resistant top layer.

The structure beneath the roof too

As a structural contractor, we look beyond the roof covering. If a roof has been leaking for a long time, the water has often also damaged the concrete roof slab, the roof edge or the upstands. We repair those first, so the new roof sits on a sound structure. That way you solve the problem in one go, with one point of contact.

Our approach

How we tackle your roof

A new roof covering only lasts on a sound substrate and with well executed details. That is why we always start with a thorough inspection.

  1. 01

    Inspection and leak detection

    We inspect the roof covering, the connections and the drains, and trace the spot where the water gets in.

    Concrete testing and inspection
  2. 02

    Repairing the substrate

    We first repair damaged concrete roof slabs, roof edges and upstands, so the new roof sits on a solid base.

    Concrete repair
  3. 03

    Insulating where needed

    A renovation is the ideal moment to insulate a flat roof. The insulation goes under the new roof covering.

  4. 04

    New roof covering

    Depending on the roof and its use, we choose bitumen, EPDM or a liquid roofing system that seals seamlessly around every detail.

  5. 05

    Details and drainage

    We carefully finish roof edges, upstands, penetrations, drains and emergency overflows, because that is where most leaks occur.

  6. 06

    Checks and handover

    We check watertightness and document what has been done, so you know exactly what is on your roof.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about roofing

Your question is not listed? Ask us directly.

Can a leak in a flat roof be repaired without renewing the entire roof?

Often, yes. If the roof covering is otherwise in good condition, we repair the leak or the detail where the water gets in. If the roof is worn out or there is water under the membrane, a renovation is the better choice in the long term.

Which roof covering is best for a flat roof?

No single system is best for every roof. Bitumen is robust and proven, EPDM is flexible and resistant to sun and frost, and a liquid roofing system seals seamlessly around penetrations and details. We advise based on your roof, its use and your budget.

How long does a new flat roof last?

A properly installed flat roof lasts for many years, provided the details are well executed, the roof is checked regularly and the drains are kept clear.

Can the building stay in use during the works?

Yes. We work in phases and make the roof watertight at the end of each stage, so activities inside can carry on as normal.

How much does a roof renovation cost?

That depends on the surface area, the condition of the substrate, the insulation and the chosen system. After a site visit, you will receive a clear, free quote with no obligation.

Which region do you cover?

From Bornem we work throughout Flanders and Brussels, and on larger projects in the rest of the Benelux.
