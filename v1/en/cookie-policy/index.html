In short: this website uses no cookies and does not track you.

No cookies

We place no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies and no social media cookies. That is why there is no cookie banner and nothing for you to accept.

All fonts, photos and videos are loaded directly from our own server. No data is sent to third parties such as Google.

Like any website, our server records technical data about a visit, such as the IP address and the page requested. This happens without cookies. Read more in our privacy policy.
