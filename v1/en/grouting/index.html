Why grouting?

A machine or steel column rests on a concrete foundation via a steel plate. There is always a small gap between plate and concrete, needed to set the structure level. That gap must be filled completely, otherwise the entire load rests on a few levelling bolts or small contact points. The result is cracking in the concrete, vibration in the machine and, in time, damage to the structure. Grouting fills that gap with a flowable mortar that reaches everywhere and does not shrink.

Cement or epoxy

Cement grout is a non shrink mortar that flows like a thick liquid and cures into a strong layer with high compressive strength. It is suitable for most column bases, machines and bearings.

Epoxy grout is even stronger, bonds better and resists oil and chemicals. It damps vibration well and can take load quickly. That is why we choose epoxy under heavy, rotating machines and in industrial environments.

No voids, no shrinkage

The secret of good grouting lies in the execution. The substrate must be sound, rough and dampened beforehand. The formwork must be tight. And the grout must flow under the plate from one side until it emerges on the other, so all the air is driven out. You cannot see a void under a base plate afterwards, but it does determine how the structure carries its load. That is why we work with proven products and a fixed method.

Repairing old grout too

In older installations we often find cracked or crumbling grout under column bases and machines. Water then gets in, the anchors rust and the support deteriorates. We carefully remove the old grout, check the anchors and the substrate and pour again. That gives the structure full, durable support once more.

Our approach

How grouting works

Grout must flow under the plate without voids and must not shrink after curing. That requires the right product and careful execution.

  1. 01

    Removing old grout

    We break out loose and damaged grout down to sound concrete, without damaging the base plate or anchors.

  2. 02

    Preparing the substrate

    The concrete is roughened, cleaned and dampened beforehand, so the grout bonds well and does not lose water to it.

  3. 03

    Placing formwork

    Watertight formwork around the base plate keeps the liquid grout in place and determines the finish.

  4. 04

    Choosing and mixing the grout

    Non shrink cement grout for most applications, epoxy grout for vibration, chemical exposure or a quick return to service.

  5. 05

    Pouring without voids

    We pour from one side until the grout emerges on the opposite side, so no air remains under the plate.

  6. 06

    Curing and finishing

    After curing we remove the formwork and finish the edges. Where needed, we protect everything with a coating.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about grouting

Your question is not listed? Ask us directly.

What exactly is grout?

Grout is a flowable mortar based on cement or epoxy, with aggregates and admixtures. It flows under a plate without vibration, fills every void and does not shrink as it cures. As a result, the plate bears on its full surface.

Cement grout or epoxy grout?

Cement grout is suitable for most column bases and machines. We choose epoxy grout for heavy vibration, chemical exposure or when the installation must be back in service quickly.

Why is ordinary mortar not enough?

Ordinary mortar shrinks as it cures and then leaves a gap under the plate. The load then rests on just a few points, resulting in cracks and vibration. Grout does not shrink and keeps full contact.

How soon can the machine be used again?

That depends on the product and the temperature. Rapid hardening grout can take load after a few hours, ordinary cement grout after a few days. We match the choice to your schedule.

Can you replace old grout?

Yes. We break out the damaged grout, check the anchors and the substrate and pour again. Where needed, we temporarily support the structure.

How much does grouting cost?

That depends on the number of base plates, their dimensions, the product and access. After a site visit, you will receive a clear, free quote with no obligation.
