When is drilling or sawing needed?

During a conversion, a new installation or a structural investigation, you often need to go through concrete. With the right technique, this is done cleanly and without damaging the rest of the structure.

Penetrations

Core drilling

Diamond drilling and sawing

Concrete, and reinforced concrete in particular, cannot simply be broken open without damage. Conventional breaking causes vibration, hairline cracks in the surrounding concrete and a lot of dust. With diamond tools we cut instead of break. The edge of the opening stays straight, the surrounding concrete remains intact and the reinforcement is cut cleanly. Because the tools are cooled with water, dust is also kept to a minimum.

Drilling: penetrations and core drilling

With a core drilling machine we drill round openings from a few centimetres to large diameters, in floors, walls and ceilings. This is how we create penetrations for pipes, cables, ventilation and drainage, exactly where they appear on the plan. We use the same technique for core sampling in concrete testing: the core is tested in a laboratory for compressive strength, composition and carbonation.

Sawing: openings with clean edges

For doors, windows, stairwells and slots we use wall saws and floor saws. Large openings are sawn out in blocks, which we lower in a controlled way and remove. The result is an opening with straight, smooth edges that is immediately ready for finishing or for fitting a frame.

Safely through a load bearing structure

An opening in a load bearing wall or floor changes the way a building carries its loads. That is why we work with your architect or structural engineer on this kind of job. Where needed, we first install props or strengthen the edge of the opening, for example with bonded reinforcement or a steel frame. That way the structure stays stable at all times.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about concrete drilling and sawing

Your question is not listed? Ask us directly.

How large can an opening be?

By sawing large openings out in sections, almost any size is possible. In load bearing walls, the stability of the structure determines what can be done. We decide on that together with your structural engineer.

Can I simply make an opening in a load bearing wall?

No. An opening in a load bearing element changes the way forces travel through the building. Such work usually requires calculations by a structural engineer, and sometimes strengthening. We carry out the works according to those calculations.

Does drilling and sawing create a lot of dust and noise?

Diamond drilling and sawing is water cooled. This keeps dust to a minimum and is much quieter than conventional breaking. We collect the slurry.

Can you cut through reinforced concrete?

Yes. Diamond tools cut through both the concrete and the reinforcement. With prestressed concrete we first check where the tendons are, because they must not be hit.

What is core drilling?

A cylindrical drilling that removes a core from the concrete. It is used to create a penetration, or to test the concrete in a laboratory for strength and composition.

How much does drilling or sawing in concrete cost?

That depends on the thickness of the concrete, the amount of reinforcement, the dimensions of the opening and access. After a site visit, you will receive a clear, free quote with no obligation.
