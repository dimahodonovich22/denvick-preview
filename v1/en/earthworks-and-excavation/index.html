Earthworks as the first step

Many repairs start underground. A leaking basement wall that needs waterproofing from the outside, a subsided foundation, a broken drain or a new connection: first, someone has to dig. Earthworks look simple, but digging too deep, too wide or in the wrong place turns a small repair into a big problem.

Our own mini excavator, even in tight spaces

With our own mini excavator we reach places that large machines cannot: back gardens, courtyards, narrow passages and even the inside of buildings. We dig precisely to the required depth, without moving more soil than necessary. Because the equipment is our own, we can start quickly and do not depend on hire companies.

Safe excavation

Underground utilities, unstable pit sides and heavy equipment near people call for a careful approach. That is why we request the location of utilities beforehand, dig carefully where needed and secure deeper pits. All our crews work to the VCA safety standard.

From digging to finishing

Because we also carry out repairs, waterproofing and road surface repair, our work does not stop at digging. The repair follows straight after the excavation, then we backfill, compact and finish. One crew, one schedule and a site that is left clean and tidy.

Our approach

How we carry out earthworks

Good earthworks are precise and safe. You cannot see what is underground, so preparation is everything.

  1. 01

    Site visit and utility plans

    We inspect the site and request the location of underground utilities, so we can dig safely.

  2. 02

    Setting out

    We set out the area and depth to be excavated precisely according to the plan.

  3. 03

    Excavation

    With our mini excavator we dig in a controlled way, even in tight gardens, courtyards and basements.

  4. 04

    Shoring

    For deeper pits we support the sides, so people can work in them safely.

  5. 05

    Removal or reuse

    Excavated soil is taken away or reused on site, in line with the rules on moving soil.

  6. 06

    Backfilling and compaction

    After the works we backfill and compact layer by layer, ready for finishing.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about earthworks and excavation

Your question is not listed? Ask us directly.

Can you dig in places that are hard to reach?

Yes. Our mini excavator fits through narrow passages and can work in gardens, courtyards and even inside buildings.

What happens to the excavated soil?

We take it away or reuse it on site. Specific rules apply to moving soil in Flanders, and we take them into account.

How do you avoid damaging underground pipes and cables?

Before we dig, we request the plans of underground utilities. Close to pipes and cables we dig carefully, by hand where necessary.

Do you also carry out the repair after digging?

Yes. Because we also carry out concrete repair, waterproofing and road surface repair, we can finish the job straight after digging.

How deep can you dig?

That depends on the soil, the space and the situation. For deeper pits we provide the necessary support for the sides.

How much do earthworks cost?

That depends on the volume, access and the removal of soil. After a site visit, you will receive a clear, free quote with no obligation.
