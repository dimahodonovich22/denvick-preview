Know first, then repair

The damage you see on the outside of concrete is often just the tip of the iceberg. A few spalled pieces can point to a local problem, but also to deterioration that has already spread across the entire element. Repairing without knowing how far the damage extends means risking doing too little, or too much. Concrete testing gives you the answer before any money is spent.

What we test

Concrete cover. How thick is the layer of concrete protecting the reinforcement? Insufficient cover is a common cause of concrete decay.

Carbonation. How deep has CO₂ from the air penetrated the concrete? As soon as carbonation reaches the reinforcement, the steel loses its protection.

Chlorides. Are chlorides present, from road salt or sea air? They attack the reinforcement locally and quickly.

Strength and hidden damage. With core samples we determine the quality of the concrete, and with measurements we detect voids and loose concrete that cannot be seen with the naked eye.

In difficult places too

Structures are not always easy to reach. A quay wall that can only be approached from the water, the underside of a bridge or a car park that stays in use: with our own equipment, from aerial platforms to pontoons, we carry out testing wherever it is needed.

From report to repair plan

Testing is only valuable if you can act on it. That is why you do not receive a pile of figures, but a clear report with photos, test results and a straightforward assessment. It comes with a practical repair plan with priorities: what needs doing now, what can wait and which technique suits best. Because we can also carry out the repair ourselves, you know straight away where you stand.

Our approach

How concrete testing works

Good testing combines what you see with what you measure. Only then can you plan a repair that tackles the cause.

  1. 01

    Visual inspection

    We map the visible damage: cracks, spalled sections, rust stains, damp patches and efflorescence.

  2. 02

    Measurements on site

    We measure the concrete cover over the reinforcement and detect voids and loose concrete.

  3. 03

    Core sampling

    At representative locations we drill cores from the concrete for further testing.

  4. 04

    Carbonation and chlorides

    We determine how deep carbonation has penetrated and whether chlorides are present, the two main causes of concrete decay.

  5. 05

    Report

    You receive a clear report with photos, test results and an assessment of the condition.

  6. 06

    Repair plan

    Based on the results, we propose a practical repair plan with priorities and possible techniques.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about concrete testing

Your question is not listed? Ask us directly.

What does concrete testing measure?

Depending on the question: the concrete cover over the reinforcement, the depth of carbonation, the presence of chlorides, the strength of the concrete and the extent of visible and hidden damage.

What is carbonation?

CO₂ from the air slowly penetrates the concrete and lowers its alkalinity. When that zone reaches the reinforcement, the steel is no longer protected and can corrode. A simple test on a core or a fracture surface shows us how deep the carbonation goes.

Do core samples leave damage?

No. The holes are small and are neatly filled with repair mortar after drilling.

How long does testing take?

The work on site usually takes one or a few days. The analyses and the report follow after that. You will be given a clear timeline in advance.

Will I also get advice on the repair?

Yes. The report contains not only the results but also a practical repair proposal with priorities. That way you can plan and budget the works.

How much does concrete testing cost?

That depends on the size of the structure, the number of measurements and core samples, and access. After an initial conversation, you will receive a clear, free quote with no obligation.
