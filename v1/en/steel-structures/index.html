Strong, light and fast

Steel has exceptional strength relative to its weight. A steel girder spans large distances with limited depth, and a steel structure can be installed quickly because the components are made to measure in advance. That is why steel is often the best choice for renovations, extensions and strengthening, especially when a building stays in use.

From small to large

Beams and portal frames. Anyone who wants to remove a loadbearing wall to create an open space needs a steel beam to take over the load. We install it safely, with props, and provide solid bearings.

Supports and strengthening. Steel columns, girders and frames strengthen an existing structure or support a new load, such as a machine or an installation on the roof.

Platforms and mezzanines. In halls and warehouses, a steel mezzanine floor creates extra space without extending the building.

Stairs and railings. Steel stairs, railings and balustrades, functional or as an architectural feature.

Always based on a calculation

A loadbearing steel structure is structural work. Which section is needed, how the connections are made and how the structure bears follows from a calculation by a structural engineer. We work closely with your architect and engineer, or put you in touch with an engineering firm.

Design, fabrication and installation from one source

Because we also drill, saw, grout and repair, we take on the entire process. The same crew makes the opening, installs the steel structure, grouts the bearings and finishes everything neatly. That means less coordination for you and one party responsible from start to finish.

Our approach

From design to installation

A steel structure must fit to the millimetre and carry its load safely. That is why we combine accurate measuring with a calculation and careful assembly.

  1. 01

    Survey and design

    We survey the situation and work out the structure in detail, together with your architect or structural engineer.

  2. 02

    Calculation

    Sections, connections and bearings are calculated for the loads the structure has to carry.

  3. 03

    Fabrication

    The components are made to measure and prepared, so that assembly on site goes smoothly.

  4. 04

    The steel receives a protective treatment suited to indoor or outdoor use.

  5. 05

Frequently asked questions

Questions about steel structures

Your question is not listed? Ask us directly.

Can you install a steel beam to replace a loadbearing wall?

Yes. We first install props to support the building, make the opening and place the steel beam according to the structural engineer's calculation. The bearings are then grouted and the wall is finished.

Is a calculation needed?

For loadbearing steel structures, yes. The engineer determines which section is needed and how it must bear. If you do not yet have an engineer, we will put you in touch with an engineering firm we work with.

How is steel protected against rust?

Depending on the environment, with a primer and paint coats or by galvanising. Indoors, a simpler system than the one used outdoors or in damp conditions is often sufficient.

Do you take on small jobs too?

Yes. From a single beam or railing to a complete structure for a hall. Every job receives the same care.

How long does installation take?

Because the components are made to measure in advance, installation is quick. We usually install a steel beam for a loadbearing wall in one or two days.

How much does a steel structure cost?

That depends on the dimensions, the sections, the finish and the installation. After a site visit, you will receive a clear, free quote with no obligation.
